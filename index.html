Immich to Gphoto Transfer

A private, self-hosted tool that keeps a second backup of a personal Immich photo library in the owner's own Google Photos account. It runs only on the owner's home server and is used by its owner only.

What it does with Google

The tool asks for the drive.file permission for one purpose: to read the storage usage counter of the signed-in Google account (Google Drive API, about.get → storageQuota). It uses this number to warn its owner before the account runs out of storage.

  • It does not read, list, download, modify or delete any photos, videos or Drive files.
  • It does not create files in Google Drive.
  • No data is sent to any third party.
